# Country expansion notes

This version expands the CRM with additional funders and partnership leads in Spain, Switzerland, Italy, Belgium, the Netherlands, France, Denmark/Nordics, Australia, Japan and New Zealand.

## How to use these records

Many of these funders do not behave like simple open-call grantmakers. Treat them as one of three routes:

1. **Open call** - apply through a published portal.
2. **Partner route** - identify a local NGO, university or hospital partner first.
3. **Research lead / verify-current** - use the record as a lead, but verify current programmes before outreach.

## Suggested positioning for medical, psychological and technology-addiction prevention projects

- Use public-health language, not only product language.
- Show measurable outcomes: reduced problematic use, improved family functioning, improved sleep, school engagement, wellbeing measures.
- Prepare data protection and safeguarding notes, especially for youth and health-related data.
- For Europe, include GDPR, DPIA thinking and ethical review if research is involved.
- For research funders, find an academic partner and include evaluation methodology.
- For country-specific funders, work with a local partner instead of applying as a foreign standalone project.
