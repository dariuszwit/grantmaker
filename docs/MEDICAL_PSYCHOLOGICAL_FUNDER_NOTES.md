# Medical, psychological and addiction-related funders

This version adds a stronger medical/psychological layer:

- Wellcome
- NIDA / NIH addiction and digital therapeutics
- SAMHSA behavioral health and substance-use grants
- PCORI mental and behavioral health opportunities
- EU health funding calls
- Robert Wood Johnson Foundation
- Brain Canada
- Bell Let's Talk
- Mind UK
- Maudsley Charity

Recommended positioning for a technology-addiction / digital wellbeing application:

1. Do not describe it only as an app.
2. Describe it as an evidence-informed prevention or intervention pathway.
3. Add clinical/psychological advisors.
4. Define target group: youth, parents, schools, churches, clinics, recovery communities.
5. Clarify safety, privacy, consent, safeguarding and escalation protocols.
6. Define outcomes: reduced problematic use, improved sleep, family functioning, anxiety/depression metrics, treatment engagement, relapse prevention or school wellbeing.
7. Prepare a 1-2 page concept note and a research/evaluation plan.
